Mind the gap.

A ball brakes at a gap, leans forward to judge it, then backs up for a running jump. It absorbs the landing, reaches the target, and celebrates with a small hop.

Storyboard & story

Open the full digital storyboard sketch sheet

The approach establishes weight through decreasing bounces. A controlled stop and forward lean reveal the obstacle. The retreat and crouch prepare the jump; a stronger landing squash and smaller rebounds resolve it. A brief celebratory hop finishes the story before the ball settles.

Animation principles

Squash and stretch

The three sampled poses show incoming stretch, impact compression, and recovery. The large landing compresses more than the smaller impacts. Airborne stretch follows the direction of travel, with compensating sideways scale to preserve volume.

Compare all three poses

Anticipation

The ball leans toward the gap, rolls back, and compresses before accelerating into the leap. These actions establish what it is about to attempt.

Arcs and timing

An annotated composite places five movie poses on the same background, 0.25 seconds apart. A dotted guide traces the arc. The smaller vertical spacing near the apex shows the ball slowing on ascent, then speeding up on descent. Passive rebounds retain 65% of the preceding vertical speed.

Motion & materials

The animation uses translation, deformation, and rotation keys, with no Maya dynamics. Flights follow parabolic paths. During moving contacts, the ball continues traveling and turning as it compresses. Rolling brakes smoothly, and the impact deformation decreases with each rebound.

The main leap lasts 1.1 seconds. The ball’s deliberate retreat, launch, and celebration are stylized character actions; the shared gravity and consistent rebound timing give its airborne motion weight.

Coral rubber and cream seams contrast with teal platforms. Brass edge strips mark the gap, and a green target marks the finish. Brighter fill light, taller grounded platforms, and a closer fixed camera keep the action legible.
